#### `cli/engine/engines/browser/detect-url.mjs`: URL scans
  - **Surfaces off the ancestor chain.** Every `low-contrast` verdict, not only the SAFE_TAGS path's, also asks the structural layer climb (`impeccable_core::browser::visual::layer_under_text`), which answers below the fold and through layers with `pointer-events: none`, which `elementsFromPoint` never lists. Besides pictures and fills, the climb reads a gradient that paints a surface on a box that is nobody's ancestor (not a dot grid, hairline, small cell, mask, a wash at alpha times opacity of 0.1 or less, or a box at opacity 0.3 or less) and an SVG shape drawn around the text (at most three times the text box plus 16px on each axis) as paint the walk never read; an ancestor painting an opaque gradient ends the climb as its fill does. A positioned `::before` or `::after` is the surface only where its box, placed from its containing block, pixel offsets, size and transform (a rotation, scale or skew as the bounds of the transformed box about its centre), covers the text; one it cannot place keeps the size test. A pseudo-element or box at a negative `z-index` is skipped where an opaque fill or gradient lies between it and its stacking context (the host's own fill included, unless the host opens that context): an offset shadow behind a white card, a `-z-10` layer inside a white section that is not `isolate`. Paint the capture cannot place for certain (an unplaced pseudo, a pseudo with no recorded `z-index` or a negative layer with no recorded `isolation` over such a fill) is read as before but decides no verdict of its own: the verdict stands outside the SAFE_TAGS path, nothing is reprinted against it, and `dark-glow` ignores it. The snapshot records `isolation`, and `zIndex` and `translate` for pseudo-elements. Outside the SAFE_TAGS path the climb's own finding counts where the stacks cannot answer (below the fold, a replay that never asked), already see unread paint, or confirm the walk only because the layer ignores pointer events or is an SVG shape. That paint is read for the surfaces it can make over the ground the walk read (`unread_verdict`): the climb is asked again past each translucent layer, up to four, until it reaches paint that hides what is beneath or the walk's ground; the walk's own translucent layers are placed over or under that paint by the box each hangs from. A verdict that fails on every surface in that span stands, also where the stacks see unread paint, and prints `N:1 (need T:1); text #ink on #surface (layer on <box>)` against the surface it reads best on when the walk named a flat colour more than 24 away from the span and the span's colours are known (fills and gradients); a verdict that passes on every one is dropped; one that depends on what the paint shows (a photo, unknown SVG fill) stands outside the SAFE_TAGS path, and the SAFE_TAGS path waives it as before. Outlined text (a `-webkit-text-stroke` wider than 0 in a colour of its own, or two or more opaque text-shadow layers blurred at most 2px and offset in opposing directions) keeps its element verdict. The snapshot records `webkitTextStrokeColor` and `webkitTextStrokeWidth`. `dark-glow`'s "on dark background" form reads an opaque detached fill or gradient under the element's box as its surface; a picture or translucent layer leaves the claim as the walk made it.
  - **What the visual pass refuses to read.** The JS reported whatever its sampling produced; the engine says nothing where the reading is not the text's own background. The candidate collector applies the element pass's text gates before a candidate takes one of the `maxCandidates` slots, is sampled, or is scrolled into view: text with no letter and no digit (`◯`, `{ }`, `/`) as well as emoji-only text; a disabled control (`[disabled]`, `[aria-disabled="true"]`); a computed font size under 1px; a `color` at alpha 0.02 or less, or a transparent `-webkit-text-fill-color`, unless the element clips its own background to its text; and an element the painted-at-capture predicate (`crates/core/src/browser/painted.rs`, text gate) says is not painted, such as a tab-strip cell past its clipping edge or a carousel slide parked beside the page. A background image read as a surface has to be under the text: an image drawn `no-repeat` (from the computed `background` shorthand's first layer) whose painted rect, at a `background-size` the capture can place (pixels, percentages, `cover`, or any size once the image's intrinsic size is loaded), covers neither the candidate's text box nor its own box (`body` and `html` are asked only about the text) ends the walk `background image does not cover the text` (`stop`), whether or not the pass could load the file. An icon sprite framing a count and a mark beside a button label are refused this way; a repeating tile and a placement the capture cannot state are read as before. In the stack walk (`impeccable_core::browser::visual`): a translucent surface no longer restarts the walk at its own parent (which re-entered the stack above the node it came from and composited that surface over itself until its alpha read as opaque, and which skipped siblings painted between the surface and that parent); the walk keeps descending the one `elementsFromPoint` stack, collecting translucent surfaces until an opaque one grounds them, then composites them back in order (topmost surface still names the method, `...+alpha`); a walk that reaches the end of the stack with nothing opaque is unresolved rather than reporting the last translucent color as a surface. A gradient answers analytically only when its stops agree: any stop below 0.95 alpha (a scrim, a glow, a `from-x/10` wash composites differently at every point of the box) or two opaque stops more than 2× apart in contrast make it `gradient stops disagree`, and an `svg` in the stack makes it `<tag> paint`; both end the walk (`stop`) rather than letting the nodes under them answer for the text, so the candidate falls to the screenshot pass. In the screenshot flow: a candidate whose reasons include `background-clip text`, `filter`, or `backdrop filter` is refused before the two screenshots are taken; the verdict is the median over the *glyph cores* (channel delta ≥ 90% of the strongest delta in the box, ≥ 8 of them; when fewer than 8 pixels reach 90%, as on small or thin text, the pixels from 75% up) rather than the tenth percentile over every changed pixel, which antialiased edges drive to 1:1; and the pass reports nothing when the diff is not a glyph over its background; more than 55% of the clip changed (the page repainted), more than a quarter of the changed pixels moved the opposite way from the rest (text mid-animation leaves its old position and its new one in the same diff), most fully painted pixels are not the color the text declares (`preferRenderedForeground` aside, where the painted value *is* the claim), the surface under the glyphs stands 2:1 or further from the surface beside them in the same box (the page paints its own text twice, so hiding one copy leaves the other where the glyphs were), or the median of every measured pixel is more than 3× the verdict (the sample is two populations, not one surface). The snippet shape is unchanged. `median` in it is the median over the same glyph cores the verdict reads, so it prints the verdict's number and never reads below it (it used to be the median over every changed pixel, edges included, which printed verdicts above their own median such as `pixel contrast 3.5:1 median 1.7:1`). The median over every changed pixel still decides the bimodal refusal above, but it is not printed.
  - **Round 11 contrast: the pixel pass and the gates before it** (corpus run 1004; URL engine unless noted). The pixel pass still hides a candidate's `text-shadow` with its glyphs. Where that reading is refused as a box repainted between captures, the candidate is not outlined (the `text outline` reason or routing), and every layer of its `text-shadow`, composited over its ink, sits at 2:1 or more from the ink (`haloSeparates` on the candidate), the pass takes a second reading with the shadow kept and reads the glyphs over it; a glow in the ink's own colour, a drop shadow under grey ink, and a layer without its own colour are never kept. A `backdrop-filter` on a box whose own `background-color` is at least 0.8 opaque (and under 0.95, where the existing "under an opaque fill" reason applies) gives the reason `backdrop filter under a near-opaque fill`, which the pixel pass reads and the sampled pass refuses; the reason walk continues past that box; a backdrop filter under a thinner fill still blocks both. In `low-contrast`'s element pass, the hit-test stacks take the innermost box between the text and the walk's host whose fill is at least 0.95 opaque as the surface the text sits on (the colour scored is unchanged: the walk still folds such a fill over what lies below). On the link and span path a surface in exactly the ink's colour keeps its verdict only where the full pass's guard does not hold (the stacks confirm the surface and the page ground carries no picture), the surface is not the page ground, and the text is the whole visible content of the `a`, `button` or `[role=button]` around it (the control's text is the element's text, and no other box in it that does not hold the element is an `svg`, `img`, `picture`, `canvas`, `video`, `i`, `object` or `embed` or paints a fill, a background image or a border; inside the element, an `svg`, `img`, `picture`, `canvas`, `video`, `object` or `embed` counts whatever text it holds, and any other such box counts only when it holds no text; a box with no rendered area or at `visibility: hidden` or `collapse` never counts); every other such pair is dropped, and the static engine still drops every one. A box at an opacity of 0.02 or less whose only animation setting `opacity` is running once on the document timeline, holds its end frame (`forwards` or `both`), uses `replace` composition, and ends within 60 seconds of delay plus duration counts at its end frame's opacity wherever the Round 10 scroll-held opacity counts (the opacity gate and fold, the Text and Box paint gates, `painted_at_capture` and the page checks that ask it, the text-box and shown-across tests). A box below full opacity whose only animation setting `opacity` is `infinite`, paused, and has a keyframe at 0.1 or less is not at rest: the opacity fold does not blend it and the pixel pass does not read it. Text whose computed font size is at least 120px, or at least three times the page's largest `h1` and at least 80px, whose settled effective opacity (a scroll-held box, or one a timed entrance moves at capture at any captured value, at its end frame; a box whose opacity an animation or transition moves toward an end the capture cannot read, or that a paused loop holds, is not faint) times its colour's alpha is 0.25 or less, which is not a control's whole label, and whose words run together equal a site name (the host name without `www.`, with and without its last label; `og:site_name`; `application-name`; where neither meta names the site, the title when it has one part between `|`, `·`, `•`, `-`, `–` and `:`, and otherwise only the parts whose letters and digits, lower-cased and run together, are 3 or more characters inside the host name's labels without its last one, read the same way) or open one word for word, is a decorative wordmark (taste call r3-04's advisory); the static engine reads no layout and never applies it.
  - **Round 12 contrast surfaces** (corpus run 1006; URL engine unless noted). *dark-glow.* A shadow layer cast with an offset whose HSL lightness is under 0.25, whose relative luminance is under the surface's, and whose hue is within 50 degrees of a chromatic surface's lifts it by nothing (shade, not light); every other layer, a zero-offset halo and a dark shadow in a hue of its own included, keeps the channel distance. Where the walk under the element ends on an ancestor gradient whose stops are all opaque (no `url()` layer with it), the lift test runs against each stop and the glow is passed over only where it lifts none of them to the floor; a translucent stop leaves the floor to decide alone, as before. *Stack walk.* A box whose `background-clip` (or `-webkit-background-clip`) clips every layer to its text paints no surface for the text of the box holding it: the walk reads it as `no readable background` and goes on beneath it. A translucent pixel of a `url()` background is laid over the same box's own `background-color` (alpha over 0.05) before the walk goes on, with method `canvas-background-image+fill`: opaque where the fill is, still translucent (the two composited) where it is not. The in-page bundle's `vc_css_url_finish` takes the node handle first. *Gradient text.* Text whose glyph fill is transparent (`color` at alpha 0.02 or less, or a transparent `-webkit-text-fill-color`) under `background-clip: text` with a gradient whose stops are all opaque and readable (no `url()` layer) is scored by its best stop against the surface under its parent, `N:1 (need T:1); gradient text, best stop #stop on #ground`, and reports only where even that stop fails. The surface has to be one flat colour the text-surface walk resolves from the parent (no gradient host or samples, no background image on the host), the structural climb from the parent over the text has to answer with an ancestor's own fill (from the element itself its own clipped gradient answers first, and a dark backdrop laid beneath it is never seen), the stacks must not see it covered, and the glyphs carry no `text-shadow` or outline. Near-bar ratios report as advisory, and decorative text as advisory. Gradient grounds are not scored (taste call r10-t4).
### PART 2: skill/scripts verbs
- **`comp-spec`**; turns an approved comp into a measured build spec. `--schema` prints the region-map JSON schema without reading or writing a project. `--comp <png> --regions <json> --inspect-map` writes reference-only crops, overlay and report in a new directory; exit 2 means diagnosed hard errors, exit 1 means command/input failure. Masked assets also produce paginated, comp-derived `comparison-N.png` sheets (six original/checker pairs per image, matched scale and aspect ratio), listed by `COMPARE` lines and the JSON `comparisonSheets` array. Every affected instance is retained; sheets do not change matcher inputs or approvals. Partial foreground masks are warnings naming the excluding regions and remain in the inspector’s main inspection queue even with zero hard errors. The CLI summary counts them separately; they do not imply an asset failure or visual approval. Code rectangles exclude their full overlap, so separate text elements need separate bounds. Mapping metadata (`parentId`, `reviewGroup`) survives measurement. Review groups must contain peer code regions of one kind and container status; mixed groups and grouped raster assets are refused both at measurement and at component-review preparation. `--comp <png> --grid` writes `.impeccable/build/comp-grid.png` (10x10 labeled grid) and prints PALETTE/BANDS/NEXT; `--comp <png> --regions <json>` measures regions into `.impeccable/build/spec.json` (region box, sampled palette, medium, aspect, detail energy, plate path for raster kinds) and prints the spec; `--comp <png> --auto [--out <draft.json>]` writes approximate bands to a new draft file (default `.impeccable/build/regions.draft.json`), without modifying the measured spec or build state; `--print` prints the compact spec; `--crop <id> [--out f] [--scale n] [--raw]` writes a reference crop; `--plate-prompt <id>` prints the regeneration prompt. `--spec <path>` overrides the spec path (default `.impeccable/build/spec.json`). Validation refusals (stderr, exit 1) are the JS strings verbatim: a region with no id / duplicate id / no note (bands included) / a missing or unknown kind, a `box` that is not numeric, leaves the 0..1 frame, or rounds below one comp pixel, a code-kind region whose note names painted material, a code region over 25% of the comp, a grid span that is not `<colrow>:<colrow>`, uncovered ink cells without `allowUncovered`. Every `text`, `control` and `chrome` region (containers included) gets `surface: {flat, rules}` and, when any 64px window of its comp crop reads as painted (pixels inside raster regions left out), `flags: [{"id":"painted-pixels","message":...}]` plus a `FLAG <id> painted-pixels: <message> <agent advice>` line in the printed spec; docs/PLAN-REVIEW.md defines both readings. The flag is never a refusal, and `codeDrawn` does not suppress it. A raster region whose note names a frame and then the view it opens onto gets `flags: [{"id":"baked-composite","message":...}]` and its own `FLAG` line (docs/PLAN-REVIEW.md). Apart from these additions, spec.json is byte-identical to the JS output.
